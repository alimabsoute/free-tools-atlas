The Top 10, ranked by leverage

Picked for how often they'll touch your real work — not novelty. These pay for themselves the first week.

1
Squooshsquoosh.app
Your mobile-first mandate is bundle < 1MB, WebP, lazy-load. Squoosh compresses to WebP with live before/after, offline, free. Image weight is also a direct Core Web Vitals / SEO ranking factor — this one tool serves every site you ship.
2
Pexelspexels.com
Already the backbone of the ForkFox media pipeline. No-attribution photos and video — the legal, free engine behind hero media for Dish/Carte and every marketing page.
3
Photopeaphotopea.com
Full Photoshop in-browser, opens PSDs. The fix for the recurring logo problem — crop transparent padding (the Caposeo bbox issue), composite heroes, export retina assets with zero install.
4
Perplexityperplexity.ai
Cited AI search — your fast lane for restaurant facts (ForkFox), competitor/SERP intel (Caposeo) and product enrichment (Launch Sentinel) without burning Anthropic API quota you've already hit ceilings on.
5
Crontab.gurucrontab.guru
ForkFox runs 3×/day; Launch Sentinel runs 10 crons; JobBoard chains GHA stages. One mis-typed cron expression = a silent dead pipeline. This decodes any schedule instantly.
6
LanguageToollanguagetool.org
A free, self-hostable grammar/style layer to bolt onto the ForkFox anti-slop bar — automated, no hand-editing (your hard rule), catching passive/clunky prose before publish.
7
Archive.pharchive.ph
Snapshot every restaurant/source page you cite in ForkFox → permanent dead-link insurance for your external-linking rule, and frozen competitor pages for Caposeo audits.
8
HaveIBeenPwnedhaveibeenpwned.com
After the Firecrawl key-leak incident, breach hygiene isn't optional. Pair with VirusTotal + privnote (share secrets safely instead of inline) as standing ops practice.
9
Lucidelucide.dev
One consistent, MIT-licensed icon set across BoardLord, PhynxTimer, Caposeo, Moonlight — kills the mismatched-icon look and ships clean with React.
10
Ray.sowww.ray.so
Gorgeous code screenshots for the claude-skills-100 showcase, the cheatsheet repo, and social posts. Carbon.now.sh is the alt. Instant credibility on a content page.

Security & Ops

Hygiene (post-leak)

After the Firecrawl key-leak incident — make this standing practice

  • HaveIBeenPwnedCheck every account email against known breaches.
  • VirusTotal + URLVoidScan files/URLs before trusting any dependency or link.
  • PrivnoteShare a secret once, self-destructing — never paste keys inline.
  • SimpleLogin + temp-mailAlias emails / throwaway inboxes to test your own SaaS signups.
  • WhoerSee exactly what your deployed apps leak about a visitor.
